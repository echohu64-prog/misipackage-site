FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Custom Packaging FAQs

Have questions about custom packaging? Find answers to common questions below, or contact our team to discuss your project.

Can I customize the size and design of my packaging?

Yes. We work with brands on custom packaging designs, including box dimensions, materials, printing and finishing options. Share your requirements with us to discuss your project.

What information do I need to request a quote?

Please share your packaging type, approximate order quantity, dimensions, material preferences and artwork or reference images if available. This helps us understand your requirements.

Can you work with my existing artwork or design?

Yes. You can share your existing artwork, design files or reference images for our team to review as part of your custom packaging project.

Can I request a sample before placing a bulk order?

Sample options, availability and costs can be discussed based on your packaging requirements. Contact us to confirm the options for your project.

How do I get started with a custom packaging order?

Contact us through WhatsApp with your packaging requirements. Our team can discuss the design, specifications, quantity and quotation with you.
