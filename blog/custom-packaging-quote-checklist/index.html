How to Request a Custom Packaging Quote: A B2B Checklist

Publisher: MisiPackagePublished November 10, 2026

Prepare a clear custom packaging quote request with product dimensions, quantity, artwork, finish preferences, sample needs and destination details.

A useful custom packaging quote starts with a clear brief, not just a box name. Two requests for the same packaging format can involve different product arrangements, artwork, materials and order quantities.

For US and international B2B buyers, the goal is to give the supplier enough information to review the same project you have in mind. Separate requirements from preferences, and identify any decisions that are still open.

Before you request a quote: six essentials

  • Product dimensions: measurements, units and the intended arrangement.
  • Estimated quantity: the quantity for each size or artwork version.
  • Material and finish preferences: required choices, preferences and undecided items.
  • Artwork or logo: available files and their approval status.
  • Sample requirements: what you need to review before bulk production.
  • Destination country: where the order needs to go.

Identify the packaging format

Choose the closest starting format, or send a reference and explain what you want to change:

  • Sliding drawer boxes — specify the sleeve, tray and insert arrangement.
  • Magnetic rigid gift boxes — describe the lid, presentation and interior layout.
  • Hanging vape boxes — include the retail fixture and product-retention requirements.
  • Windowed hanging vape boxes — mark the side-window area and what should remain visible.
  • Cardboard display boxes — provide the intended unit count and available counter space.
  • Custom Mylar bags — explain pouch sizing, usable fill space and closure preferences.
  • Custom tube packaging — describe product fit and opening requirements. Identify any child-resistant documentation requirements before selecting a design.

A reference image helps explain direction, but it does not establish material composition, dimensions or tested performance. Confirm those separately.

Provide product dimensions and the intended arrangement

Distinguish the product from the package

Send the product’s length, width and height, or its widest dimensions and overall height where that is more useful. State the units. Identify whether any supplied box measurements are internal or external.

For several items, include a simple arrangement sketch and label each component. Note areas that need clearance, support or access. Do not assume a photograph supplies the measurements needed for a quotation.

State quantity, materials and finish preferences

Separate estimated quantities from confirmed requirements

Provide the estimated order quantity and identify different sizes, designs or product variations. Make clear whether the quantity is a total or applies to each version.

Minimum order quantities vary depending on the packaging style, materials, and production requirements. Contact us for a project-specific quotation.

Explain the appearance you want

List known material requirements and finish preferences. If the material is undecided, say so and include visual references. Ask for the proposed construction to be identified in the quotation; a matte appearance or metallic finish alone does not specify the material.

Share artwork and sample requirements

Attach the available logo, artwork or design reference and identify what is final. Ask which file formats and preparation steps are required before artwork approval; do not assume every supplied file is ready for production.

Explain what you need to evaluate in a sample: structure, product fit, materials, artwork or the overall presentation. Samples are available to help you review the packaging structure, materials, and design before moving forward with bulk production. Confirm what the proposed sample will represent.

Include the destination country and planning needs

Give the destination country and any known delivery-location details. Ask what the quotation includes for transport and which arrangements still need confirmation. Do not assume freight, duties or a delivery commitment are included unless stated.

If you have a target date, share it as a requirement for review rather than treating it as an agreed delivery date.

Production lead time depends on the product specifications, order quantity, and customization requirements. A timeline will be confirmed during the quotation process.

Use a brief that another buyer can understand

The following is an illustrative brief structure, not a customer case study or a quoted specification:

  • Packaging format: preferred format and attached reference.
  • Product dimensions: attached measurements, units and arrangement sketch.
  • Estimated quantity: quantity for each size or artwork version.
  • Materials and finishes: required choices, preferences and undecided items.
  • Artwork: logo or file attached; approval status identified.
  • Sample requirements: the details the sample needs to demonstrate.
  • Destination country: delivery location and any target date for review.

Before comparing quotations, check that they describe the same quantity, construction, artwork scope and sample requirements. Request clarification wherever an assumption could change the order.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need final artwork to start the discussion?

Share the artwork or logo you have and identify unfinished items. Confirm the required files and approval steps before moving to production.

What minimum quantity should I expect?

Minimum order quantities vary depending on the packaging style, materials, and production requirements. Contact us for a project-specific quotation.

Can I review a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes. Samples are available to help you review the packaging structure, materials, and design before moving forward with bulk production.

How long does production take?

Production lead time depends on the product specifications, order quantity, and customization requirements. A timeline will be confirmed during the quotation process.
