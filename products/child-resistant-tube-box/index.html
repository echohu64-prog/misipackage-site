Custom Tube Packaging for Child-Resistant Packaging Projects

Custom tube packaging offers a cylindrical presentation for products that need a different format from a rectangular carton. MisiPackage supports project discussions around tube dimensions, paperboard, printed branding and opening requirements for OEM and wholesale orders.

MADE AROUND YOUR PROJECT

Tube Fit, Paperboard Options and Opening Requirements

Usable Diameter and Product Height

Provide the product height, widest points and any accessories to be packed with it. Distinguish usable interior space from the tube exterior so the proposed fit can be reviewed accurately.

Explain how the product should be positioned and removed. Include a reference photo or drawing to show features that may affect insertion, access or the interior arrangement.

Paperboard and Printed Presentation

Discuss paperboard, specialty textures, coatings, custom printing, foil stamping and embossing for the tube exterior. The exact material composition and construction must be confirmed for the project.

Mark the main branding, product-information areas and preferred artwork orientation. Review the curved surface and opening areas together when planning the printed layout.

Printed Branding and Project Requirements

Identify the branding and product-information areas on the curved surface. Describe the required opening method so the printed layout can be reviewed alongside the proposed construction.

If child resistance is required, request applicable testing or certification documents for that specific configuration and target market. Do not treat a structure sample or visual resemblance as proof of compliance.

PREPARE A CLEAR BRIEF

Quote Preparation Checklist

A tube quotation needs a clear dimensional brief and an explicit statement of any opening or compliance requirements.

Tube Fit and Opening Brief

Send product height, widest dimensions and reference images. Label any target tube measurements as internal or external, and describe the opening behavior you want reviewed.

Branding and Required Evidence

Provide artwork or a logo, paperboard and finish preferences, and the target market. Specify any child-resistant requirements and documents needed for your review.

Quantity, Samples and Country

Include the estimated order quantity, sample requirements and destination country. Explain what must be checked on the sample and which requirements need documentary confirmation.

Child-Resistant Tube Box FAQs

Which measurements are needed to review tube fit?

Send the product height and widest dimensions, plus measurements of any accessories. If you specify a tube size, distinguish the usable interior from the external diameter and height.

Does every tube use the same opening mechanism?

Do not assume a particular opening method. Describe your requirements and ask to review the proposed mechanism and construction for the exact tube configuration before approving the order.

What evidence should I request for a child-resistant project?

Request supporting documents that apply to the proposed opening mechanism, construction and intended market. The tube name, a photograph or a sample alone does not demonstrate tested child-resistant performance.

How should I brief the interior arrangement?

Show how the product will sit in the tube and how it should be removed. Include accessory positions and any support requirements so the proposed interior construction can be evaluated rather than assumed.

How do I specify the paperboard and printed exterior?

Share material or texture references, artwork and desired finish areas. Paperboard, coatings, foil stamping and embossing can be discussed; confirm the actual material composition and finish combination for the selected tube.
