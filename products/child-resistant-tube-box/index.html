Child-Resistant Vape Tube Packaging FAQs

How does a button-lock tube closure work?

The push-to-lock button closure works by pressing the button to engage the locking mechanism. The closure structure can be customized according to your tube design and packaging requirements.

Can I order custom paper tube sizes with a button-lock closure?

Yes. Tube dimensions, button-lock construction and interior arrangements can be discussed based on your product measurements and packaging requirements.

Can custom vape paper tubes include printed logos and special finishes?

Custom printing, brand artwork, matte finishes, foil stamping and embossing can be discussed according to your preferred design and production requirements.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Minimum order quantities vary depending on the packaging style, materials and production requirements. Contact us for a project-specific quotation.

Can I request a sample before bulk production?

Yes. Samples are available to review the tube structure, button-lock operation, materials, interior fit, and printed design before moving forward with bulk production.

Does a button-lock paper tube automatically qualify as child-resistant packaging?

No. The presence of a button-lock closure by itself does not establish child-resistant compliance. The finished package must be assessed against the applicable requirements for its intended market and use.

Can you provide child-resistant testing or certification documents?

Testing, certification, and documentation requirements must be discussed and confirmed for the exact tube construction and destination market. Share the applicable standard and required evidence before quotation or sample approval.

How is production lead time determined?

Production lead time depends on the confirmed tube specifications, order quantity, customization, and any agreed testing or approval steps. The applicable timeline will be confirmed during quotation.
