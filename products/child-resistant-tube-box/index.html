Button-Lock Vape Tube Packaging FAQs

How does a button-lock tube closure work?

The push-to-lock button closure works by pressing the button to engage the locking mechanism. The closure structure can be customized according to your tube design and packaging requirements.

Can I customize the tube size and button-lock structure?

Yes. Tube dimensions, button-lock construction and interior arrangements can be discussed based on your product measurements and packaging requirements.
