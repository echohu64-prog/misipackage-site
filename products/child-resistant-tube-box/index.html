MADE AROUND YOUR PROJECT

Tube Structure, Branding and Opening Requirements

Tube Structure and Internal Fit

Provide the product’s widest dimensions, overall height, and reference images. Explain how the product should sit inside the tube and whether it needs an inner support or separate space for additional components.

Review the usable interior and opening arrangement together rather than relying only on the external tube dimensions.

Paperboard and Printed Branding

Discuss paperboard, specialty textures, surface coatings, custom printing, foil stamping, and embossing. Share artwork showing the intended branding and any areas required for product information or opening instructions.

Material composition and structural specifications should be confirmed for the proposed design.

Opening Requirements and Supporting Documents

For a child-resistant packaging project, state your destination market and documentation requirements before approving the structure.

Request opening and closing instructions and ask which supporting documents apply to the exact configuration being proposed. Any claim of tested or certified child-resistant performance requires relevant supporting evidence.

Sampling and Order Planning

Review product fit, the proposed opening arrangement, materials, and artwork during the sampling process. Confirm the specification before approving bulk production.

Production lead time depends on the product specifications, order quantity, and customization requirements. A timeline will be confirmed during the quotation process.

Tube Packaging FAQs

How does the tube open and close?

The opening mechanism must be confirmed for the proposed design. Request operating instructions as part of the specification review.

What dimensions are needed for a quote?

Provide the product’s widest dimensions and height, along with reference images and any internal support requirements.

What if my project requires child-resistant packaging?

Include the destination country and relevant project requirements in your brief. Ask about documentation for the specific design before making a purchasing decision.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Minimum order quantities vary depending on the packaging style, materials, and production requirements. Contact us for a project-specific quotation.

Can I review a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes. Samples are available to help you review the packaging structure, materials, and design before moving forward with bulk production.
