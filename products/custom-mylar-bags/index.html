MADE AROUND YOUR PROJECT

Flexible Pouch Construction, Closures and Printing

Flexible Pouch Construction and Usable Space

Start with the target pouch width and height, then explain the usable space required for the contents. If you have an existing package, drawing, or reference photo, include it with your inquiry.

For a resealable pouch, review the product space together with the zipper position, tear notch, and sealing area. The final dimensions should be considered as one complete layout rather than as external measurements alone.

Zipper Pouch Packaging and Closure Choices

Explain whether the pouch will be filled by hand or as part of a production process, how it should be opened, and whether it needs to be resealed after use. These details help identify the closure arrangement to evaluate.

Material composition, sealing performance, and any application-specific requirements must be confirmed for the proposed construction. If your project requires food-contact, barrier, or child-resistant documentation, mention this at the quotation stage so the relevant requirements can be reviewed.

Custom Printed Pouches and Finishes

Share your logo, artwork files, preferred colors, and any required product-information areas. You can also describe the visual direction you want your packaging to communicate.

Matte, glossy, and metallic appearances can be discussed as finish preferences. The available treatment should be confirmed against the selected material and pouch construction before production.

Custom Mylar Bag FAQs

Which measurements should I provide?

Send the target external pouch dimensions and explain the required fill space. Include your preferred layout or a reference package so usable dimensions can be confirmed.

Does zipper pouch packaging require heat sealing?

The required filling and final-closure process must be confirmed for the proposed pouch and intended use. Include your sealing requirements in the quotation brief.

What information is needed to review material suitability?

Describe the intended contents, destination country, and any application-specific documentation requirements. These should be reviewed alongside the proposed material construction.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Minimum order quantities vary depending on the packaging style, materials, and production requirements. Contact us for a project-specific quotation.

Can I review a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes. Samples are available to help you review the packaging structure, materials, and design before moving forward with bulk production.
