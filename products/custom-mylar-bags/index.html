Custom Printed Mylar Bag FAQs

Can I order custom size Mylar bags?

Provide the target external dimensions and explain the usable fill space needed for the intended contents. Include product quantity per pouch, a filling-method reference and any existing package or drawing so the layout can be reviewed.

Can I get custom resealable bags with a logo, zipper and tear notches?

Yes. Include those features in the brief so their positions and the proposed pouch format can be reviewed together. Confirm the filling and final-closure process, and check opening and resealing on a sample before approval.

Which material and performance details need confirmation?

Describe the intended contents, filling process, destination country and any required documentation. Food-contact, barrier or child-resistant requirements need separate project review; a pouch format or visual finish does not establish those properties.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Minimum order quantities vary depending on the packaging style, materials, and production requirements. Contact us for a project-specific quotation.

Can I review a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes. Samples are available to help you review the packaging structure, materials, and design before moving forward with bulk production.
