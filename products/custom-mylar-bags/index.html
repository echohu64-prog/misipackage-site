Flexible Pouch Construction, Closures and Printing

Flexible Pouch Construction and Usable Space

Provide the target pouch dimensions and explain how much usable filling space your product needs. Include reference images or an existing package when available.

For resealable Mylar bags, consider the product space together with the zipper, tear notch, and proposed seal areas.

Zipper Pouch Packaging and Closure Choices

Share how the pouch will be filled, opened, and used. This helps define the closure requirements to review.

Material composition and sealing requirements should be confirmed for the proposed pouch. Any food-contact, barrier, or child-resistant requirement needs separate supporting documentation.

Custom Printed Pouches and Finishes

Discuss logo printing, artwork placement, and matte, glossy, or metallic finishes. Identify important brand elements and product-information areas so they can be reviewed against the proposed pouch layout.

Consider the material, closure, finish, and artwork together when preparing a wholesale Mylar bag quotation.

Sampling and Production

Use the sample review to examine the proposed pouch structure, materials, design, and closure arrangement before approving bulk production.

Custom Mylar Bag FAQs

Which measurements should I provide?

Send the target external pouch dimensions and explain the required fill space. Include your preferred layout or a reference package so usable dimensions can be confirmed.

Does zipper pouch packaging require heat sealing?

The required filling and final-closure process must be confirmed for the proposed pouch and intended use. Include your sealing requirements in the quotation brief.

What information is needed to review material suitability?

Describe the intended contents, destination country, and any application-specific documentation requirements. These should be reviewed alongside the proposed material construction.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Minimum order quantities vary depending on the packaging style, materials, and production requirements. Contact us for a project-specific quotation.

Can I review a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes. Samples are available to help you review the packaging structure, materials, and design before moving forward with bulk production.
