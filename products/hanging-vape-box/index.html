Hanging Vape Box FAQs

What fixture information should I provide?

Send hook dimensions, fixture photographs, and any retailer-supplied packaging requirements. These help define the hanging feature to review.

How will the device be held inside?

Internal structures can be discussed as part of customization. Submit device measurements and the intended orientation so the proposed retention arrangement can be reviewed.

Can I submit existing artwork?

Yes. Share the artwork or logo with the packaging brief so its placement can be reviewed against the proposed carton.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Minimum order quantities vary depending on the packaging style, materials, and production requirements. Contact us for a project-specific quotation.

Can I review a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes. Samples are available to help you review the packaging structure, materials, and design before moving forward with bulk production.
