MADE AROUND YOUR PROJECT

Hanging Carton Structure, Product Retention and Printing

Hanging Tab and Retail Fixture

Provide the hook or fixture reference used by your retailer, including available measurements or photographs. The hanging area should be considered alongside the carton width and intended display orientation.

Do not assume one tab layout fits every fixture. Ask to review the proposed package on the intended display before approving the structure.

Paperboard and Surface Selection

Discuss paperboard, textured surfaces and matte coatings for the hanging carton. Share the visual direction you want and confirm the material choice against the proposed structure.

A finish preference does not replace a fit review. Check the proposed material and internal arrangement with the device in its intended hanging position during sampling.

Printed Panels and Retail Information

Custom printing, foil stamping and embossing can be discussed for the carton. Identify which elements use each requested treatment and confirm their placement against the proposed panel layout.

Mark the front-facing branding, product information and hanging area on the artwork reference. Keep important text clear of folds and the tab area when reviewing the layout.

BEFORE BULK PRODUCTION

Sampling and Production Planning

Sampling should check how the carton hangs as well as how the device fits inside it.

Check a Loaded Sample

Samples are available to review structure, materials and design before bulk production. Check the proposed carton with the device inside and on the intended fixture where possible.

Agree on Quantity and Timing

MOQ varies by carton style, material and production requirements. Production timing depends on the order quantity, specifications and customization; request a project-specific schedule with the quotation.

PREPARE A CLEAR BRIEF

Quote Preparation Checklist

Help the quotation team understand both the device and the retail setting in which the carton will be used.

Device and Fixture References

Send device dimensions, accessory details and the planned hanging orientation. Include a hook reference or retailer requirements so the tab can be reviewed against the fixture.

Artwork and Surface Preferences

Supply a logo or artwork, required product-information areas and paperboard or finish preferences. Identify which panel needs to face shoppers on the display.

Order and Sample Requirements

State the estimated quantity, sample review needs and destination country. Mention whether a device and fixture reference can be supplied for fit checks.

Hanging Vape Box FAQs

What retail hook information should I provide?

Send the retailer fixture specification if available, or clear photographs and measurements of the hook. Include any constraints on package width or hanging position so the proposed tab layout can be reviewed.

How is device retention planned inside a hanging carton?

Provide the device shape, dimensions and intended orientation. Identify any movement you want to address. The proposed internal structure should then be checked with the actual product during sampling.

Which panel should carry the main branding?

Identify the surface facing the shopper when the package is hanging. A fixture reference helps coordinate the front panel, side information and hanging area before the artwork is finalized.

Can one hanging box be used for several device models?

Share measurements and component details for every model. Each fit needs review; a box developed around one device should not be assumed to accommodate a different model without checking it.

What should I check when reviewing a hanging box sample?

Check the device fit, intended orientation, product access and artwork placement. Where possible, place the loaded sample on the intended fixture to review the hanging tab and front-facing presentation.
