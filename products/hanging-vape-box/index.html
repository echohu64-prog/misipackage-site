Custom Hanging Vape Box FAQs

What fixture information should I provide?

Send hook dimensions, fixture photographs, and any retailer-supplied packaging requirements. These help define the hanging feature to review.

How will the device be held inside?

Internal structures can be discussed as part of customization. Submit device measurements and the intended orientation so the proposed retention arrangement can be reviewed.

Can I submit existing artwork?

Yes. Share the artwork or logo with the packaging brief so its placement can be reviewed against the proposed carton.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Minimum order quantities vary depending on the packaging style, materials, and production requirements. Contact us for a project-specific quotation.

Can I review a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes. Samples are available to review the carton structure, hanging tab, product fit, materials, and printed design before moving forward with bulk production.

Can the hanging tab be customized for a specific retail fixture?

Share the hook or pegboard dimensions, fixture photographs, and retailer requirements. The hanging feature and carton construction can then be reviewed together for the intended display setup.

What is the difference between a standard hanging vape box and a windowed hanging box?

A standard hanging box uses a printed carton presentation without a product viewing window. A windowed hanging box includes a specified viewing area; see our Windowed Hanging Vape Box page for that format.

How is production lead time determined?

Production lead time depends on the confirmed carton specifications, order quantity, printing and finishing requirements, and sample approval. The applicable timeline will be confirmed during quotation.
