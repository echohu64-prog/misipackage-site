Custom Cardboard Countertop Display FAQs

How many products can a cardboard display box hold?

Provide the packaged dimensions of each sale unit, product weight information and the expected unit count. Add a sketch showing the preferred rows and orientation. Capacity is reviewed for the proposed structure rather than assumed from a display image.

Can compartments be planned for several product variants?

Compartment arrangements can be discussed as part of the display brief. List each variant, its packaged dimensions and the quantity to show, then mark how the products should be grouped. Confirm the proposed layout with a loaded sample.

What size should my countertop display box be?

Send the available width and depth, plus any height restriction or nearby fixture details. Check that the proposed cardboard counter display leaves room for shoppers to see and remove products in the intended retail position.

Can cardboard counter displays be supplied flat or assembled?

The supplied format depends on the agreed structure and must be confirmed during quotation. Explain your storage, assembly and retail setup requirements, then review the proposed process with the sample before approving production.

What should I check on a countertop display sample?

Load the intended product mix and review the compartment arrangement, footprint, customer access and refilling process. Check the printed panels both when the display is full and as stock is removed, and confirm the proposed construction before placing the bulk order.
