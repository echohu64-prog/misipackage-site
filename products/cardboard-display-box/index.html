Custom Cardboard Counter Display Boxes for Retail Products

A cardboard display box organizes multiple sale units in a shared retail presentation. MisiPackage offers custom counter display packaging with tailored dimensions, internal arrangements and printed branding for B2B projects.

Begin with the packaged item, planned unit count and available counter or shelf space. Those details help define the footprint, product arrangement and visible branding before the display construction is selected.

MADE AROUND YOUR PROJECT

Display Capacity, Compartments and Retail Branding

Plan Around the Sale Unit

Provide the dimensions of the product as it will be displayed, including its individual packaging. State the intended number of units and whether the display will carry one SKU or several.

Use a layout sketch to show rows, orientation and access. Planned capacity needs review against the actual product dimensions and weight; the photograph is not a capacity specification.

Paperboard, Kraft and Surface Choices

Paperboard, kraft and textured surfaces can be discussed for your display project. Share material references and confirm the selected construction against the planned contents and retail layout.

Review the proposed display with the actual products before approving its working capacity. Material appearance alone does not establish how the loaded structure will perform.

Printed Panels and Visible Branding

Use custom printing to distinguish the display from the individual products it holds. Foil stamping or embossing can be discussed for selected branding areas and confirmed against the proposed panel layout.

Identify the panels that should remain visible when the display is full and as products are removed. Provide artwork for the proposed header and front-facing areas as applicable to the agreed layout.

BEFORE BULK PRODUCTION

Sampling and Production Planning

A display sample is most useful when evaluated with the planned mix of products and the available retail space.

Confirm Capacity and Layout

Agree on sale-unit measurements, planned quantity per display, SKU arrangement and the available counter footprint before requesting a sample.

Review Access and Refill

Samples are available to review structure, materials and design before bulk production. Check the proposed layout with the intended contents, including removal, refilling and visible branding.

Confirm the Production Plan

MOQ varies with display style, materials and production requirements. Production lead time depends on the specification, order quantity and customization; confirm the schedule during quotation.

PREPARE A CLEAR BRIEF

Quote Preparation Checklist

Separate the number of displays you want to order from the number of products each display should hold.

Sale Units and Retail Space

Send packaged product dimensions, weight information, planned units per display and SKU details. Include available counter width, depth and any height limits.

Layout and Printed Panels

Provide a layout reference, logo or artwork, and paperboard or finish preferences. Mark compartment needs and the branding areas that should face the shopper.

Display Quantity and Samples

State the number of displays to quote, sample requirements and destination country. Explain whether product access, refilling or the supplied assembly format needs specific review.

Cardboard Display Box FAQs

How should I specify the capacity of a counter display?

Give the packaged dimensions of each sale unit and the number you want to display. Include product weight information and a proposed arrangement so the working layout can be reviewed rather than inferred from a photo.

What information is needed for a display with several SKUs?

List each variant, its packaged dimensions and the planned quantity. Show how you want the products grouped and identify any compartment requirements for separating or organizing them.

Which counter measurements matter for a display quotation?

Provide the available width and depth, plus any height restriction. Include relevant shelf or surrounding fixture details so the proposed footprint and printed areas can be reviewed in context.

Will the display be supplied flat or assembled?

Confirm the supplied format for the proposed structure during quotation. Include storage, assembly and retail setup requirements rather than assuming a format from the product image.

What should a loaded display sample be checked for?

Check the product arrangement, customer access, refilling process and visible branding with the planned contents. Review the proposed capacity and construction before approving the bulk order.
