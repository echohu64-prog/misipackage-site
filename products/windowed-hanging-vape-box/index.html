Custom Side-Window Hanging Vape Box FAQs

When should I choose a windowed box instead of a standard hanging carton?

Choose a windowed format when showing part of the product is a priority. A standard hanging carton may suit a brief that calls for uninterrupted printed panels. Compare the viewing area, product positioning and protection requirements before selecting the structure.

Is the side window an open cutout or a covered window?

The construction needs to be confirmed for the proposed carton. State whether your project requires an uncovered or covered opening so the available approach can be reviewed. The image does not establish a window material or performance specification.

How should I specify product positioning and retention?

Send device measurements, the intended hanging orientation and a marked photo showing the feature to display. Describe any insert or internal-support needs, then check the proposed arrangement with the device inside a sample.

Where should branding go around the window?

Keep essential text and logos on the printed panels, clear of window edges, folds and the hanging area. Review the artwork in the intended display orientation so the product view and branded panels support the same presentation.

What should I review on a windowed hanging box sample?

Review the side-window view, device alignment, internal arrangement, printed panels, and hanging position with the intended product. Where possible, check the sample on the retail fixture and confirm the window construction before approving the final specification.

Can the side window be customized to show a specific product feature?

Yes. Provide a marked product photo or drawing and identify the feature you want shoppers to see. The viewing area and its position can then be reviewed alongside the device dimensions, carton folds, and internal support.

Can the side window be covered with a transparent material?

State whether you prefer an uncovered opening or a covered window. The available construction and material need to be confirmed for the specific carton design and sample; do not assume a window material from the product image.

How is production lead time determined?

Production lead time depends on the confirmed carton specifications, order quantity, customization, and sample approval. The applicable timeline will be confirmed during quotation.
