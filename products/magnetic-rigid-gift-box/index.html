Custom Magnetic Rigid Boxes for Vape Products

A magnetic rigid box presents the product beneath a lid that closes magnetically. For vape brands, the packaging brief needs to connect that opening experience with the product arrangement, printed panels, and intended presentation.

MisiPackage offers custom magnetic rigid boxes for vape products with dimensions, insert layouts, and finishes reviewed for each project. Whether you are planning a single product or a coordinated set, start with the contents and how they should appear when the lid opens. Share product measurements, a layout reference, and your artwork direction so the proposed box construction can be considered before quotation and sample review.

MADE AROUND YOUR PROJECT

Magnetic Closure, Inserts and Printed Finishes

Magnetic Closure and Rigid Box Construction

Magnetic closure packaging should be planned around how the lid opens and how the contents are presented. Show the preferred opening direction and identify the surface that should face the buyer.

Provide product measurements and label any target box dimensions as internal or external. The proposed lid, usable interior, and insert arrangement need to be reviewed together before the dimensions are finalized. Include any storage, assembly, or packing-line requirements in your initial brief. Confirm the supplied format and closure construction for the proposed box rather than assuming these details from a photograph.

Inserts and Interior Arrangement

For rigid boxes with inserts, list every item that will be packed, including accessories. Provide individual measurements and a sketch showing the preferred arrangement. A coordinated set should be evaluated as a complete layout. The position of one component may affect the space available for another, the visible presentation, and access to the contents.

Identify which item should be seen first when the lid opens and where space is needed to lift each component out. Confirm the proposed insert material and construction during project review. Use the intended contents when checking fit, positioning, and removal on the sample.

Exterior Wraps, Printing and Finishes

For custom printed rigid boxes, identify the lid, side panels, and interior areas that need artwork. Supply a panel reference showing logos, product information, and any design that continues between surfaces. Consider the closed exterior and the opened presentation separately so important branding is visible in the intended position.

Share paper samples or reference images to communicate your preferred texture, surface finish, and overall appearance. Specialty papers, foil stamping, and embossing can be discussed alongside the proposed printed design. Mark the areas intended for each treatment. Confirm the material and finish combination for the selected construction before approving the production specification.

PREPARE A CLEAR BRIEF

Preparing a Magnetic Rigid Box Quotation

For wholesale magnetic rigid boxes, a useful brief describes the complete package rather than only its outside size. If you have a target box size, state whether it is internal or external. Reference images can help explain the presentation you want, while the proposed construction and specifications are confirmed separately.

Product Measurements and Lid Reference

Provide product dimensions and measurements for every included accessory, plus an interior arrangement sketch and preferred lid-opening direction.

Artwork, Paper Wraps and Finishes

Share paper wrap, material, and finish preferences. Supply logo or artwork files, with exterior and interior panels identified.

Quantity, Samples and Destination

State the estimated order quantity, identifying separate sizes or artwork versions. Include sample requirements and the details you need to evaluate, the destination country, and any relevant packing or storage requirements.

Magnetic Rigid Box FAQs

Can one magnetic rigid box be planned for several components?

Provide the dimensions and preferred position of every component. The proposed insert and box layout can then be reviewed as a complete set, including the presentation and access needed for each item.

How should artwork be divided between the lid and interior?

Identify each surface to be printed and provide a panel reference. Show which artwork should be visible with the box closed and which should appear when the lid opens, including requested finish areas.

What should I check about the magnetic closure during sample review?

Review how the lid opens, closes, and aligns in the proposed assembly. Check it with the intended contents and insert in place, and raise any adjustments before approving the final packaging specification.

Will the box be delivered flat or assembled?

The supplied format must be confirmed for the selected construction during quotation. Include storage, assembly, and packing-line requirements in your brief; do not assume a flat or assembled format from the product image.

What should I compare between magnetic box quotations?

Compare the proposed dimensions, insert arrangement, printed surfaces, finishes, sample scope, and supplied format using the same brief. Ask for clarification where different assumptions could affect the packaging you receive.
