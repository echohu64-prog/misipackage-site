Magnetic Box Construction and Presentation

Magnetic Closure and Interior Layout

The lid construction and magnetic closure shape how the box opens and closes. Inside, the insert and layout determine how the product is presented.

Provide the dimensions of every item and a reference showing the intended arrangement. For a multi-item package, identify which components need separate spaces and which should be seen first when the lid opens.

Wraps, Printing, and Finishes

Explore specialty papers, textured surfaces, custom printing, foil stamping, and embossing. Consider the lid, sides, and interior artwork together when preparing your design.

Customization discussions can cover box dimensions, lid construction, product inserts, artwork placement, and decorative finishes.

Review the Presentation Before Bulk Production

Use the sample review to assess product fit, opening and closing, insert placement, and the selected finish. Confirm the final construction and artwork before approving the order.

Production lead time depends on the product specifications, order quantity, and customization requirements. A timeline will be confirmed during the quotation process.

Magnetic Gift Box FAQs

Can the box include an insert?

Inserts are part of the current customization options. Send product dimensions and the preferred arrangement for review.

Can one box present multiple items?

Share the dimensions and position of each item so the available interior configuration can be reviewed for your project.

Will the boxes arrive assembled?

Confirm the supplied format and any assembly requirements for the quoted construction. Include these details when comparing storage and freight needs.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Minimum order quantities vary depending on the packaging style, materials, and production requirements. Contact us for a project-specific quotation.

Can I review a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes. Samples are available to help you review the packaging structure, materials, and design before moving forward with bulk production.
