Magnetic Closure, Interior Layout and Printed Finishes

Lid Opening and Magnetic Closure

Explain how the lid should open, how the box will be presented and which surfaces need branding. A reference package or drawing helps communicate the intended opening experience.

The closure details should be confirmed for the proposed construction. Do not select a package from appearance alone; review the opening and closing action during sampling.

Papers, Textures and Finishes

Specialty papers and textured surfaces can set the visual direction of the exterior wrap. Provide material references and explain how the package should coordinate with your existing branding.

Discuss foil stamping and embossing with the paper selection. Confirm the chosen finish combination and review the material appearance during sampling.

Wrap Artwork and Printed Presentation

Coordinate custom printing across the lid and side panels. A reference showing the box both open and closed helps clarify where the artwork continues and which interior surfaces need a printed treatment.

Supply separate artwork references for the lid, exterior panels and any printed interior areas. Mark the intended logo placement and finish details so they can be reviewed as one coordinated presentation.

PREPARE A CLEAR BRIEF

Quote Preparation Checklist

For a magnetic gift box quote, show the full presentation rather than only the outside dimensions.

Lid, Wrap and Finish Artwork

Order and Approval Needs

Magnetic Rigid Gift Box FAQs

Can a magnetic gift box be planned around a multi-item set?

Share the dimensions and preferred placement of every item, including accessories. The interior layout and insert can then be reviewed together rather than estimated from the main product alone.

How should I describe the lid and magnetic opening experience?

Provide a drawing or reference showing the opening direction and how you want to present the box. Confirm the proposed closure details and review their operation on the sample.

What should I check on a magnetic box insert?

Check the position of each item, the visible presentation when the lid opens and whether items can be lifted out comfortably. Review the proposed insert material and construction with the intended contents.

Will the magnetic box arrive assembled or flat?

Confirm the supplied format for the selected construction during quotation. Do not assume a flat or assembled format from the exterior photograph; include storage or packing-line requirements in your brief.

Can the lid and interior use different artwork?

Identify each surface you want printed and supply a clear panel reference. Review the relationship between exterior branding and the opened presentation before the artwork is approved.
