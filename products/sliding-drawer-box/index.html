MADE AROUND YOUR PROJECT

Sleeve-and-Tray Packaging, Inserts and Printed Finishes

Sleeve, Tray and Product Access

Sleeve and tray packaging should be planned as one assembly. Provide the dimensions of the vape product and any accessories, then show how they should sit inside the tray.

Mark the opening direction and the areas needed to lift each item out. Review the sleeve, usable tray space, and proposed insert together before confirming the box dimensions.

Paper Stocks and Finish Preferences

Share paper, texture, and finish references for the sleeve and tray. Foil stamping and embossing can be discussed alongside the proposed materials and artwork.

Confirm the material and finish combination for the selected construction. Share reference images or samples to help communicate your preferred paper texture, surface finish and overall packaging appearance.

Artwork Across the Sleeve and Tray

Identify the surfaces that need printing, including the closed sleeve, visible tray, and any interior areas. Mark logos, product information, and requested finish details separately.

Consider what remains visible when the drawer is closed, partly opened, and fully extended so the artwork follows the intended opening experience.

Custom Drawer Vape Box FAQs

Should I provide product dimensions or a finished box size?

Start with the dimensions of the product and accessories. If you have a target box size, label it as internal or external so the sleeve, tray, and insert can be reviewed together.

How should I describe the drawer opening direction?

Use a sketch or reference photograph. Mark the pull-out direction, the main branded face, and how the product should appear when the tray is opened.

What information is needed for an insert layout?

List every item, its dimensions, and its preferred position. Identify where space is needed for removal. The insert material and construction must be confirmed for the proposed arrangement.

Do the sleeve and tray need separate artwork references?

Yes. Identify each surface you want printed, including visible tray and interior areas. Reviewing the panels together helps show where artwork may be concealed by the sleeve.

What should I check on a drawer box sample?

Review the sleeve fit, drawer movement, insert positioning and product access using the intended contents. Check the printed surfaces with the drawer both closed and open before approving the final production specifications.
