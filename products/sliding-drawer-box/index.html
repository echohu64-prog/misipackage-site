MADE AROUND YOUR PROJECT

Drawer Box Structure, Materials and Ordering

Sleeve, Tray, and Insert Construction

Start with the dimensions of the product and how it should sit in the tray. Include reference images showing its shape, orientation, and any components that need separate spaces.

For drawer boxes with inserts, explain which parts of the product should remain visible and accessible. The proposed sleeve, tray, insert, and opening details can then be reviewed as one structure.

Materials, Printing, and Customization

Discuss paper stocks, specialty materials, custom printing, foil stamping, and embossing. Coordinate the sleeve artwork with the tray and interior presentation to create a consistent package.

Your customization brief can cover dimensions, insert layout, artwork placement, and finish preferences. If you have an existing package, include photographs and identify what you want to retain or improve.

Samples and Production Planning

Use the sample review to check the proposed structure, product fit, materials, and design before approving the bulk order.

Production lead time depends on the product specifications, order quantity, and customization requirements. A timeline will be confirmed during the quotation process.

Sliding Drawer Box FAQs

What measurements should I provide?

Send the product dimensions first. If you already have a target box size, include it and identify whether those measurements are internal or external.

Can the drawer box include an insert?

Custom inserts are listed options. Share the product shape, dimensions, and preferred arrangement so the proposed insert can be reviewed.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Minimum order quantities vary depending on the packaging style, materials, and production requirements. Contact us for a project-specific quotation.

Can I request a child-resistant drawer design?

Identify child resistance as a project requirement before selecting the structure. Ask about the proposed opening mechanism and supporting documentation for that specific design before approving the order.

Can I review a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes. Samples are available to help you review the packaging structure, materials, and design before moving forward with bulk production.
