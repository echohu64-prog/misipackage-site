Custom Sliding Drawer Boxes and Product Inserts

A sliding drawer box combines an exterior sleeve with a tray that pulls out to reveal the product. MisiPackage offers custom dimensions, insert options and printed finishes for brands planning wholesale packaging orders.

Start with how the product should sit, which components need separate spaces and how the buyer will remove them. Those decisions shape the tray layout, sleeve artwork and opening experience before a quotation is prepared.

MADE AROUND YOUR PROJECT

Sleeve-and-Tray Construction, Inserts and Finishes

Sleeve, Tray and Product Fit

Provide the product dimensions and its intended orientation inside the tray. Distinguish the space needed for the product from any target external box size; the sleeve, tray and insert must be reviewed together.

Include a sketch showing the drawer opening direction and which surfaces should face the customer. A reference package can help explain the proportions and opening feel you want to evaluate.

Paper Stocks and Finish Selection

Discuss paper stocks and specialty materials for the sleeve and tray. Confirm the proposed materials against the insert layout and the intended presentation rather than selecting them only by appearance.

Share texture or surface references along with your preferred foil stamping or embossing areas. The material and finish combination should be agreed on for the selected construction.

Printing Across the Sleeve and Tray

Use custom printing to coordinate the closed sleeve with the visible tray. Submit artwork with logos, text and finish areas identified for review against the proposed box layout.

Identify artwork for the sleeve, tray and visible interior separately. Indicate logo placement and finish areas so the branding can be considered across the closed package and the opened drawer.

BEFORE BULK PRODUCTION

Sampling and Production Planning

Use the sample stage to review the complete drawer assembly, rather than the printed sleeve in isolation.

Confirm the Complete Assembly

Agree on product orientation, tray layout, insert requirements and the sleeve artwork before requesting a sample. Flag any components that will be packed together.

Review Drawer Movement

Samples are available to review the structure, materials and design before bulk production. Check the drawer movement, insert fit and access to each item with the intended contents.

Confirm the Order Plan

MOQ varies with the drawer construction, materials and production requirements. Production lead time depends on the specifications, quantity and customization; confirm both during quotation.

PREPARE A CLEAR BRIEF

Quote Preparation Checklist

For a useful drawer box quotation, show how the product and the moving parts should work together.

Product and Tray Measurements

Send product dimensions, accessory measurements and a layout sketch. Label any proposed box dimensions as internal or external and show the preferred opening direction.

Sleeve Artwork and Finishes

Provide a logo or artwork reference for the sleeve, tray and interior. List paper and finish preferences, with marked areas for foil stamping or embossing if requested.

Quantity, Samples and Destination

Include estimated order quantity, sample requirements and destination country. Note which items will be available for checking the tray and insert during sampling.

Sliding Drawer Box FAQs

Should I send product dimensions or the finished drawer box size?

Send the product dimensions first, including accessories. If you have a target box size, identify it as internal or external. The usable tray space, insert and sleeve need to be evaluated as one assembly.

How should I specify the drawer opening direction?

A simple sketch or reference photo is useful. Mark the pull-out direction, the face carrying the main artwork and how the product should appear when the tray opens.

What information helps define a drawer box insert?

List each item in the tray, its dimensions and the preferred arrangement. Explain where the customer needs access to remove it. The insert material and construction should be confirmed against that layout.

Can I check the sliding action before approving bulk production?

Yes. Sampling is supported. Review the proposed sleeve, tray and insert with the intended product, checking how the drawer moves and whether the contents can be removed comfortably.

Do the sleeve and tray need separate artwork planning?

Identify every surface you want printed, including any visible tray or interior areas. Reviewing these panels together helps avoid artwork being hidden by the sleeve or interrupted when the drawer opens.
