HELP US UNDERSTAND YOUR PROJECT

Details to Include in Your Quote Request

01 / PRODUCT

Packaging Format and Dimensions

Tell us which format you are considering, such as a drawer box, magnetic rigid box, hanging carton, button-lock tube or custom printed pouch. Include target dimensions, product fit and any reference photos or drawings.

02 / MATERIALS

Material and Finish Preferences

Share material preferences, surface finishes and any structural requirements. If you need specific performance properties or compliance documents, identify them so they can be reviewed for the proposed construction.

03 / BRANDING

Artwork and Printing

Attach your logo, artwork, color references or a design brief. Let us know whether you have front-and-back artwork, multiple versions or finishing details to discuss.

04 / ORDER

Estimated Quantity and Variants

Provide an estimated order quantity, number of designs or sizes, and destination country. Minimum order quantities vary by packaging style, materials and production requirements.

05 / SAMPLE

Sample and Review Needs

Tell us whether you need a sample and which details you want to check, such as dimensions, fit, opening, structure, material appearance or printed artwork. Sampling is supported.

06 / TIMING

Target Schedule

Share your target timing and any important project milestones. Production lead time depends on the product specifications, order quantity and customization requirements; timing is confirmed during quotation.
